# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming project. Contributors should communicate constructively, distinguish evidence from inference, accept good-faith correction, and make space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, project communication channels, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security advisory channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or permanently exclude a participant depending on severity and recurrence. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
